Cookies at a Glance

A simple overview of cookies and tracking technologies on DARAWAT:

  • Cookies are small pieces of information stored by a website in a user's browser.
  • Some cookies may be necessary for website functionality.
  • Other cookies may remember preferences or help improve the website.
  • DARAWAT does not need cookies for online payments because DARAWAT does not process online payments.
  • Users can manage cookies through their browser settings.
  • DARAWAT should only use optional tracking technologies when they are actually implemented and appropriately disclosed.

What Are Cookies?

“Cookies are small text files or similar browser storage technologies that websites may use to remember information about a user's visit.”

Cookies can help websites remember preferences, maintain sessions, improve functionality, understand basic website usage, and provide certain features without making them sound dangerous or intrusive.

Why DARAWAT May Use Cookies

Depending on platform features and implementation, DARAWAT may utilize browser technologies for the following purposes:

⚙️ Essential Functionality

Cookies or browser storage may be used to support features such as login sessions, account authentication, security, user preferences, and overall website functionality. Only claimed when actually implemented.

🎨 Preferences

Cookies or local browser storage may remember preferences such as language selection, accessibility settings, and display preferences. Note: The existing DARAWAT website uses localStorage rather than traditional cookies for these preferences.

📊 Analytics

“If DARAWAT uses analytics tools in the future, they may collect information about how users interact with the website so we can understand performance and improve the platform.” (No specific analytics provider is currently named as none are implemented).

📢 Advertising

“DARAWAT does not currently use advertising cookies unless otherwise stated.” This accurately reflects the project's current non-commercial discovery focus.

Types of Cookies

DARAWAT categorizes cookie technologies into distinct types:

  • Necessary Cookies: Used for essential website functions (e.g., authentication, security, session management). These may not be optional if required for the website to function.
  • Preference Cookies: Used to remember user choices like language, accessibility preferences, and interface settings.
  • Analytics Cookies: Used to understand website usage and performance (only included if analytics are actually implemented).
  • Advertising Cookies: Used to track users for advertising purposes (DARAWAT does not currently use these).

Local Storage and Similar Technologies

“DARAWAT may use browser storage technologies such as localStorage to remember certain user preferences. Browser storage is different from traditional cookies but may serve a similar purpose.”

Examples include accessibility settings (text size, high contrast mode, reduced motion) and UI preferences actually existing in the website code.

Third-Party Services

Some DARAWAT features may depend on external services such as map providers, hosting services, authentication services, APIs, or embedded content found in the provided code.

“Third-party services may use their own cookies or similar technologies. Their practices are governed by their own policies.” DARAWAT does not control third-party cookies.

Maps & Cookies

“DARAWAT may use third-party map services to provide location and map functionality. Depending on the service and implementation, the map provider may use cookies or similar technologies.”

If the website currently uses Leaflet, Leaflet itself is primarily a JavaScript mapping library, and any external tile provider or map service should be considered separately. Leaflet does not automatically track users.

Managing Your Cookies

Users can manage cookies through their browser settings:

  • Chrome: Browser Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies / Site Data
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and Site Permissions
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security
  • Safari: Settings → Safari → Privacy

“Disabling certain cookies may affect features that depend on them.”
