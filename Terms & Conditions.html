Terms at a Glance

DARAWAT allows users to discover local products and producers, view product and producer information, use location/map features, contact producers, and connect with members of the local community.

⚠️ Important Platform Model Notice

DARAWAT is a discovery and connection platform, not a traditional e-commerce checkout platform. DARAWAT does NOT provide:

  • Shopping cart
  • Checkout
  • Online payment
  • GCash/Maya payment gateway
  • Credit/debit card processing
  • Payment collection
  • Automatic transaction processing

Transactions are arranged directly between consumers and producers.

Acceptance of Terms

By accessing or using DARAWAT, you agree to follow these Terms & Conditions, our Community Guidelines, Privacy Policy, Cookie Policy, and any applicable rules and laws. If you do not agree with these terms, please do not use the platform.

About DARAWAT

“DARAWAT is designed to help consumers discover local products and producers while providing producers with an additional way to showcase their products and connect with potential customers.”

DARAWAT facilitates discovery and communication. DARAWAT does not automatically become a seller, buyer, agent, payment processor, or delivery provider simply because users interact through the platform.

User Accounts

Users are responsible for:

  • Providing accurate information and keeping account details updated
  • Protecting their password and not sharing login credentials
  • Preventing unauthorized access to their account
  • Reporting any suspicious account activity immediately

Users must strictly avoid:

  • Creating fake accounts
  • Impersonating another person
  • Using another person's account without permission
  • Providing intentionally misleading information
  • Attempting to bypass account security

If an account registration system exists, access may be subject to eligibility requirements configured by DARAWAT [Insert applicable age requirement if established].

For Producers

Producers are responsible for ensuring that their listings and profile information are accurate. They should provide accurate producer information, product names, descriptions, prices, availability, photos, categories, location information, and contact details.

Producers must not intentionally:

  • Misrepresent products or post fake products
  • Provide false prices or claim unavailable items are available
  • Upload misleading images or impersonate another producer
  • List prohibited, dangerous, or illegal products

Producers are fully responsible for complying with applicable laws and regulations related to the products or services they offer.

For Consumers

Consumers should:

  • Review product information carefully and confirm availability and price
  • Ask questions when necessary and communicate respectfully
  • Verify transaction details and exercise appropriate caution
  • Protect their personal information

Consumers must not:

  • Harass or spam producers
  • Create fake inquiries or intentionally mislead producers
  • Abuse the communication system

Product Listings

Product listings may include product names, descriptions, prices, availability, images, categories, producer information, and location details.

“DARAWAT provides tools for producers to showcase their products. DARAWAT does not independently guarantee that every listing is accurate, available, safe, or suitable for a particular purpose.” We encourage users to verify important information directly with the producer.

Transactions Between Consumers and Producers

“DARAWAT helps users discover and connect with producers, but transactions are arranged directly between consumers and producers.”

The consumer and producer are responsible for discussing product details, quantity, price, availability, pickup or delivery, payment methods, meeting locations, and other transaction specifics.

DARAWAT does not process payments, collect money on behalf of producers, provide checkout, operate a shopping cart, guarantee payment, guarantee product quality, guarantee delivery, or guarantee that a transaction will occur.

“Users should exercise appropriate care and judgment when arranging transactions with other users.”

DARAWAT Does Not Process Online Payments

“DARAWAT currently does not provide online payment processing.” There is no credit/debit card checkout, no GCash checkout, no Maya checkout, no online payment gateway, no shopping cart, and no payment collection system.

Payment arrangements are handled directly between the consumer and producer. DARAWAT does not store payment card information.

Producer-Consumer Communication

Users may communicate through available contact methods including phone, email, Messenger, DARAWAT messaging, or other methods provided by the producer. Users must communicate respectfully and avoid spam, threats, harassment, inappropriate material, scam attempts, or requests for unnecessary sensitive information.

User-Generated Content

Users may submit content including product descriptions, images, producer profiles, reviews (if implemented), reports, messages, and account details. Users must only upload content they have the right to use and must never upload copyright-infringing, illegal, malicious, fraudulent, hateful, threatening, harassing, or explicit material. DARAWAT reserves the right to review, restrict, or remove content violating platform rules.

Content Ownership

“If you upload a product photo or description, you retain your ownership rights where applicable. By submitting content to DARAWAT, you grant DARAWAT the necessary permission to display and use that content for operating and promoting the platform, subject to applicable law.”

Prohibited Activities

Users must not use DARAWAT to:

  • Commit fraud, scam users, or impersonate others
  • Harass, threaten, or abuse people
  • Distribute illegal or counterfeit products
  • Upload malicious code or attempt unauthorized access
  • Abuse platform features, spam users, or manipulate listings
  • Intentionally provide false information or violate applicable laws

“DARAWAT may take appropriate action when prohibited activity is identified.”

User Safety Guidelines

Encourage users to verify information before transactions, avoid sharing unnecessary personal information, never share passwords or OTPs, confirm the identity of the person they are dealing with, choose appropriate meeting locations, be cautious of suspicious offers, and report suspicious activity.

“DARAWAT provides a platform for connection but cannot eliminate all risks associated with interactions between users.”

Reporting Violations

Users can report fake accounts, fake products, scams, harassment, inappropriate content, incorrect information, suspicious activity, or technical problems. Reports are reviewed and appropriate action is taken.
