Quick Guides

Step-by-step walkthroughs for common platform actions.

🔍 Guide 1 — Finding a Product

  • 1
    Search: Search for a product in the search bar.
  • 2
    Browse: Explore available results and categories.
  • 3
    Select: Open the specific product or producer listing.
  • 4
    Check: Review product specifications and producer details.
  • 5
    Contact: Contact the producer directly.

🌾 Guide 2 — Finding a Producer

  • 1
    Open Producer Discovery: Navigate to producer listings.
  • 2
    Search or Filter: Filter by municipality or category.
  • 3
    Select a Producer: Choose a local producer card.
  • 4
    View Their Profile: Read about their farm or livelihood.
  • 5
    Check Their Location: View barangay and proximity map.
  • 6
    Contact the Producer: Reach out via direct communication.

📝 Guide 3 — Registering as a Producer

  • 1
    Create an Account: Sign up for a producer account.
  • 2
    Select Producer: Choose producer role status.
  • 3
    Provide Your Information: Input name, phone, and address.
  • 4
    Create Your Producer Profile: Upload farm/business info.
  • 5
    Add Your Products: List available goods and pricing.
  • 6
    Make Discoverable: Publish listings for community consumers.

💬 Guide 4 — Contacting a Producer

Find Product → Open Producer → View Contact Info → Send Inquiry → Discuss Details → Arrange Transaction

  • i
    Important Note: The actual transaction is handled directly between the consumer and producer. DARAWAT does not process orders or payments.

Having a Problem?

Quick troubleshooting solutions for common technical issues.

❌ “The website isn't loading.”
  • Check your internet connection.
  • Refresh the page.
  • Try another browser.
  • Clear browser cache if necessary.
❌ “The map isn't showing.”
  • Check your internet connection.
  • Allow location access if required.
  • Refresh the page.
  • Verify if the producer provided location info.
❌ “I can't find a product.”
  • Check your spelling.
  • Try a broader search term.
  • Browse product categories.
  • Try searching for the producer instead.
❌ “The producer information is incorrect.”
  • Report the information directly to DARAWAT support using the form below.
❌ “My account isn't working.”
  • Verify your login credentials and contact support if the problem continues.
❌ “A button or feature isn't working.”
  • Refresh the page and report the issue if it persists.
