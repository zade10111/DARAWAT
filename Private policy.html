Privacy at a Glance

A simple summary of how DARAWAT handles your data:

  • We collect information needed to provide DARAWAT's services.
  • We use information to operate and improve the platform.
  • We do not need unnecessary personal information.
  • We take reasonable steps to protect user information.
  • Users should avoid sharing passwords, OTPs, and sensitive financial information.
  • Users can contact DARAWAT about privacy concerns.

Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, DARAWAT may collect the following types of information:

Account Information

Depending on the features implemented, this may include full name, username, email address, contact number, account type, profile information, and login details.

Producer Information

For users registering as producers: producer name, business/livelihood name, product information, product descriptions, product images, location information, contact information, and producer profile details. Producer information may be displayed publicly when the producer chooses to publish it through the platform.

Product Information

May include product name, description, category, price, availability, images, and producer details. Product information may be visible to other DARAWAT users.

Location Information

Because DARAWAT uses maps and location-based discovery, location information may be used to display producer locations, help consumers discover nearby producers, improve location-based search, and display map information. Precise device location should only be requested when needed for a specific location-based feature; DARAWAT does not continuously track users.

Technical Information

Depending on the actual implementation, DARAWAT may collect technical information such as browser type, device type, operating system, IP address, website activity, error information, and basic usage information.

How We Use Information

DARAWAT may use collected information to:

  • Create and manage accounts
  • Provide DARAWAT discovery and connection features
  • Display producer and product information
  • Help consumers discover local producers
  • Provide location-based functionality
  • Respond to support and report requests
  • Improve website functionality
  • Detect technical problems
  • Protect the platform from abuse and maintain security
  • Communicate important service information

Information You Choose to Make Public

“When you create a producer profile or publish product information, certain information may be visible to other DARAWAT users.”

Examples of publicly visible details include producer names, business names, product descriptions, product images, general locations, and contact information that the producer chooses to provide publicly.

“Only publish information that you are comfortable sharing with potential customers.”

Location & Map Privacy

DARAWAT uses location information to help users discover nearby producers in San Jose de Buenavista, Antique. Location access may be requested for location-based features, and users can control location permissions through their device or browser settings. Producer locations may be displayed as part of their public profile using general barangay or municipal location information to avoid exposing unnecessary precise personal location data.

Communication with Producers

DARAWAT allows consumers and producers to connect directly. Depending on the implemented features, communication may happen through contact information, Messenger, phone, email, or DARAWAT messaging functionality if implemented. DARAWAT does not automatically process or guarantee transactions between users, and users should avoid sharing unnecessary sensitive information during communications.

Payments

DARAWAT does not currently process online payments. There is no online checkout, no shopping cart, no payment gateway, no credit/debit card processing, and no GCash/Maya checkout. Consumers and producers arrange payment and transaction details directly with each other. DARAWAT does not store payment card information.

Cookies & Similar Technologies

“DARAWAT may use cookies or similar technologies when necessary for website functionality, preferences, security, or other legitimate website operations. If cookies or similar technologies are introduced, this Privacy Policy may be updated to explain their use.”

Data Sharing

DARAWAT may share information only when necessary for legitimate platform purposes, such as information intentionally made public by users, service providers needed to operate the website, technical infrastructure providers, legal or regulatory requirements when applicable, and protection of users and platform security.

DARAWAT does not sell users' personal information.

Data Security

“We take reasonable measures to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.” This includes access controls, secure authentication practices, and regular platform maintenance. However, no online system can guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention

“DARAWAT may retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain accounts, resolve issues, comply with applicable requirements, or maintain legitimate records.”

User Rights

Users may have rights regarding their personal information, subject to applicable law and the actual capabilities of the platform. Requests may include:

  • Accessing personal information
  • Correcting inaccurate information
  • Updating profile information
  • Requesting deletion where applicable
  • Asking how information is used
  • Raising privacy concerns

“Requests may be subject to reasonable verification.”
