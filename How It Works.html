How DARAWAT Works

Discover Local. Connect Directly. Support Local.

DARAWAT makes it easier for consumers to discover local products and producers in their community. Browse available products, learn about local producers, and contact them directly to ask questions, check availability, and arrange your purchase.

Explore Local Products
THE PLATFORM PROCESS JOURNEY
DISCOVER → EXPLORE → CONNECT → ARRANGE → SUPPORT LOCAL

🛒 For Consumers

A simple guide for community consumers looking to discover local products and producers.

STEP 01

Search & Discover

Consumers browse or search for local products and producers available on DARAWAT. Note that DARAWAT helps users discover local products rather than functioning as a traditional online shopping website.

  • Search by Product
  • Search by Producer
  • Filter by Category
  • Browse by Location / Barangay
STEP 02

Explore Products & Producers

Users can select a product or producer to view complete profile details and listing information.

  • Product name & description
  • Producer name & category
  • Price & availability status
  • Photos & contact info
“Product information and availability may vary. Contact the producer directly for the latest information.”
STEP 03

Check the Location

Use DARAWAT's location and map functionality to understand where the producer is located and find nearby sources.

  • Producer barangay & address
  • General location map view
  • Distance and proximity insights
STEP 04

Contact the Producer

Instead of an “Add to Cart” or checkout button, consumers use direct communication channels labeled **Contact Producer**.

  • Ask about availability & pricing
  • Confirm product details & quantity
  • Discuss pickup or delivery arrangements
  • Messenger, Phone, Email, or Chat
STEP 05

Arrange the Transaction

Once the consumer and producer agree on the product, price, and arrangements, they coordinate directly.

  • Pickup, local delivery, or meetup
  • Direct payment handling between parties
DARAWAT does NOT process or collect online payments.
STEP 06

Support Local Producers

After successfully connecting with a producer, the consumer helps support local livelihoods and strengthens the local community economy.

Discover Local → Connect Directly → Support Local

🌾 For Producers

A clear guide for local farmers, fisherfolk, livestock raisers, and producers on joining DARAWAT.

STEP 01

Register as a Producer

A local producer creates an account and provides essential business or farm information to join the platform.

  • Producer / Business Name
  • Contact details & phone
  • Location & Barangay
  • Type of livelihood
STEP 02

Create Your Producer Profile

Build an informative and trustworthy profile showcasing your livelihood, background, and location.

  • Profile image or farm photo
  • Detailed livelihood description
  • Barangay & municipal zoning
STEP 03

Showcase Your Products

Add products you currently offer to help consumers discover your goods.

  • Product name & category
  • Description, price, & stock
  • Clear photos & availability
Purpose is to showcase and help consumers discover products, not to create an online checkout cart.
STEP 04

Get Discovered

Products and producer profiles become visible to consumers searching and filtering on DARAWAT.

  • Discovered via search terms
  • Categorized by food and goods
  • Filtered by location & barangay
STEP 05

Connect With Consumers

When an interested consumer finds your product, they reach out directly via your provided contact channels.

  • Answer product availability inquiries
  • Discuss pricing, quantities, & details
  • Coordinate pickup or meeting arrangements
STEP 06

Arrange the Transaction

Communicate directly with the consumer to agree on how and where the transaction will take place.

  • Direct terms agreement
  • Flexible fulfillment discussion
DARAWAT does not process online payments. Transaction terms are decided by you and the consumer.

What DARAWAT Does & Does Not Do

Clear boundaries ensuring transparency: DARAWAT is a discovery platform, not an e-commerce checkout store.

DARAWAT Provides

  • ✅ Product discovery
  • ✅ Producer discovery
  • ✅ Location / map information
  • ✅ Product information
  • ✅ Producer profiles
  • ✅ Search and filtering
  • ✅ Direct communication / contact
  • ✅ Local producer visibility
  • ✅ Community connection

DARAWAT Does NOT Provide

  • ❌ Online payment processing
  • ❌ Shopping cart
  • ❌ Online checkout
  • ❌ Payment gateway
  • ❌ Automatic payment collection
  • ❌ Credit / debit card processing
  • ❌ GCash / Maya checkout

Safety & User Awareness Notice

Before completing a transaction, consumers and producers should confirm the product details, price, availability, location, pickup or delivery arrangements, and other transaction details directly with each other.

DARAWAT provides a platform for discovering and connecting with local producers. Users should exercise appropriate care when arranging transactions. DARAWAT does not guarantee transactions or payments.
