About DARAWAT

Connecting Local Producers. Empowering Communities.

DARAWAT is a web-based local product and livelihood discovery platform designed to connect local producers with consumers in San Jose de Buenavista, Antique. We make discovering fresh local goods simple while strengthening our local community economy.

Explore Local Products

Our Story

The driving force behind why DARAWAT came to life.

In many communities like San Jose de Buenavista, local producers—such as farmers, fisherfolk, livestock raisers, and small agri-entrepreneurs—often face significant hurdles in reaching consumers directly. Traditional selling methods can severely limit their market reach, while consumers frequently struggle to discover nearby producers and locally available products because information is scattered or difficult to access.

We realized that community livelihoods flourish best when connections are frictionless. That is why DARAWAT was created: to provide a centralized digital platform that bridges this gap. By giving producers a digital showcase and consumers an intuitive way to explore local goods, we are keeping community commerce vibrant, transparent, and mutually supportive.

Our Mission

“Our mission is to empower local producers by providing a digital platform that makes their products more visible and accessible to consumers while helping strengthen local livelihoods and communities.”

Our Vision

“Our vision is to build a connected local marketplace where producers and consumers can easily discover, support, and grow together through technology.”
