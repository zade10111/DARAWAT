DARAWAT Community Guidelines

“A respectful community starts with everyone.”

DARAWAT is built to help local producers and consumers discover, connect, and support one another. These guidelines help keep our community safe, respectful, honest, and useful for everyone.

I Understand the Guidelines

Our Community Values

The foundational principles that guide interaction across our discovery platform.

🤝

Respect

Treat every producer, consumer, and community member with respect.

🔎

Honesty

Provide accurate information about products, prices, availability, and producer details.

🛡️

Safety

Protect yourself and others when communicating and arranging transactions.

🌱

Support Local

Use DARAWAT to help promote local products, producers, and livelihoods.

💬

Responsible Communication

Communicate clearly and respectfully when contacting other users.

🤝 Be Respectful

  • Treat others respectfully
  • Use appropriate language
  • Respect different opinions
  • Communicate professionally
  • Avoid harassment or intimidation
  • Respect personal boundaries
  • Harassment
  • Threats
  • Bullying
  • Hate speech
  • Discrimination
  • Abusive or insulting behavior
“Disagreement is okay. Harassment is not.”

🔎 Provide Honest Information

Producers should provide accurate information about their products. Do not allow users to intentionally provide:

  • Fake product information
  • Misleading descriptions
  • Fake prices
  • Fake availability
  • False producer identities
  • Misleading product images
  • False claims about products
“If a product is no longer available, update its availability or remove the listing.”

📋 Product Listing Guidelines

Listings should:

  • Use an accurate product name
  • Include a clear description
  • Provide accurate pricing when applicable
  • Use appropriate product photos
  • Indicate availability accurately
  • Select the correct category
  • Provide accurate producer/location info

Prohibited listings:

  • Illegal products
  • Dangerous goods / Stolen goods
  • Counterfeit goods / Fraudulent offers
  • Misleading ads / Inappropriate content
“DARAWAT reserves the right to review or remove content that violates community guidelines or applicable rules.”

💬 Communication Guidelines

DO When Contacting Users:

  • Introduce yourself clearly
  • Ask questions respectfully
  • Confirm product details & availability
  • Discuss price & quantity clearly
  • Agree on pickup or delivery arrangements

DON'T:

  • Spam users or send unwanted messages
  • Threaten or send inappropriate content
  • Request unnecessary personal info
  • Impersonate another person

Stay Safe When Arranging Transactions

“DARAWAT helps consumers and producers connect, but transactions are arranged directly between the users.”

Recommended Practices:

  • Confirm the producer's identity
  • Confirm product availability & price
  • Confirm quantity & pickup/delivery details
  • Meet in a safe and appropriate location when possible
  • Keep communication clear

“DARAWAT does not process online payments or act as a payment intermediary.” DARAWAT does not guarantee transactions.

🔒 Privacy & Personal Information

Protect your personal information. Users should avoid publicly sharing sensitive data.

  • Passwords & One-time passwords (OTP)
  • Bank account information
  • Credit or debit card information
  • Sensitive personal information
“Only share information that is necessary for communication or arranging a transaction. Never ask another user for their password or verification code.”

Prohibited Content

Strictly forbidden content categories across the DARAWAT platform.

🚫 Fraud & Scams

No fake offers, fraudulent activity, or attempts to deceive users.

🚫 Illegal Products

No listings for products that are prohibited by applicable laws.

🚫 Counterfeit Products

No fake or counterfeit products presented as authentic.

🚫 Dangerous Content

No content that promotes dangerous or harmful activity.

🚫 Hate & Harassment

No hateful, threatening, discriminatory, or abusive content.

🚫 Spam

No repetitive ads, irrelevant promotions, or unsolicited messages.

🚫 Impersonation

Do not pretend to be another person, producer, or DARAWAT rep.

🚫 Misleading Info

Do not intentionally provide false or deceptive information.

🌾 For Producers

Producers are responsible for:

  • Keeping product info accurate & updated
  • Providing honest descriptions & clear pricing
  • Responding respectfully to inquiries
  • Providing accurate location information
  • Following applicable laws & regulations
Do not promise products that cannot reasonably be provided.

👤 For Consumers

Consumers are expected to:

  • Communicate respectfully & ask reasonable questions
  • Respect producer availability
  • Confirm details before arranging transactions
  • Avoid harassment or spam
  • Report suspicious activity & protect personal info

🌱 Help Us Build a Better Local Community

Every user contributes to DARAWAT's community. Support legitimate local producers, provide accurate information, treat people respectfully, report problems responsibly, and help keep the platform useful.

“When we support local producers, we help strengthen our community.”

🚨 Emergency / Serious Safety Issues:
“If you believe there is an immediate threat to someone's safety or an emergency, contact the appropriate local authorities rather than relying only on DARAWAT's reporting system.” (DARAWAT is not an emergency response service).
